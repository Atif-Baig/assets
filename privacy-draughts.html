Draughts for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective [DATE] · Last updated [DATE]

This app collects nothing about you.

No account, no sign-in, no internet connection, no advertising, no analytics. The rest of this page explains that in detail.

What we collect

Nothing. Draughts is a single-player board game that runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and the app has no way to identify you.

Internet access

The app does not use the internet. It contains no networking code and requests no internet permission from Android. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What is stored on your device

One thing: your campaign progress — which rounds you have beaten and how many stars you earned.

It is kept in a private file inside the app's own storage, which no other app can read. It contains no identifier and nothing about you, and it is never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and you can clear it at any time with Reset progress on the main screen.

Nothing else is saved. Games in progress are held in memory and are gone when the app closes.

Advertising and analytics

There are none. The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics services, and no tracking or attribution software of any kind. It has no third-party libraries beyond the Flutter framework it is built with and a single Android component for saving your progress.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions at all — not storage, not location, not the camera, not contacts, not the network.

What Google collects

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect installation and crash information under its own policies, which we do not control and cannot see beyond aggregate reports. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, and collects nothing from anyone of any age.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction and deletion apply to personal information that an organisation holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request, correct or delete. If you would like your campaign progress gone, uninstalling the app or tapping Reset progress removes it completely.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
